## 1. What Service-Hub is

Service-Hub connects people who need a car repair (“Customers”) with car repair shops listed on the Platform (“Shops”). On the Platform you can:

- search for shops by name, city or service and see their hours, services, inspection fee and reviews;
- book online, at a free day and time;
- receive the quote after the inspection and accept it in full or line by line, or refuse it;
- know when your car is being worked on and when it is ready;
- message the shop;
- keep your cars in the Garage, with alerts for the roadworthiness test (ITP), liability insurance (RCA) and road vignette;
- see the repair history of each car and, if you want, buy an official history report.

**Service-Hub is not a repair shop and does not carry out repairs.** The contract for the inspection and repair is made directly between you and the Shop you choose. The Shop is responsible for the quote, the quality of the work, the parts and the warranty. You pay for the repair and the inspection fee directly to the Shop, not through the Platform. Service-Hub does not negotiate prices and does not check whether a quote is correct.

All Shops on the Platform are professionals (companies, sole traders or individual businesses) that declared at sign-up that they are entitled to provide car repair services. Your consumer rights for the repair (information, warranty, complaints) apply against the Shop.

## 3. For Customers

### 3.1 Search and how results are ordered

Shops are listed by their weighted review score: the average star rating, weighted by the number of reviews, so that a shop with few reviews does not rank above one with many good reviews just because of a single 5-star rating. Ties are broken by the number of reviews, then by name. Filters (category, city, favorites) only narrow the list; they never reorder it.

**No one can pay to rank higher.** The subscription Shops pay does not affect the order. Distance from you is shown separately (“Near you”) and as a sort option you choose; it is not part of the weighted score.

Search only shows Shops with a confirmed email and phone, at least one service selected and one open day, a subscription in good standing, and no suspension.

### 3.2 Booking

- You choose the service, day, time and car. The booking starts as **pending** and becomes **confirmed** only when the Shop accepts it. The Shop may decline the request or move it to another day or time; you are notified in every case.
- Each Shop sets its own hours, how many cars it takes per day and at the same time, how far in advance you can book and until when you can cancel. The Platform enforces these limits automatically.
- So that slots are not blocked, there are reasonable limits on active bookings and on new requests per day. If you reach a limit, the app tells you.
- When you book, the Shop receives your name, phone and language, the car details (make, model, year, license plate, VIN if you entered it) and your note. If you later edit or delete the car in your Garage, the booking keeps the details from the moment it was made.

### 3.3 Canceling and no-shows

- You can cancel in the app while the booking is pending or confirmed, up to the cancellation deadline set by the Shop (shown on its page). Please cancel as early as possible so the Shop can offer the slot to someone else.
- After the booking time, if you did not show up, the Shop may mark the booking as a **no-show**. Shops see, discreetly, when a customer has 3 or more no-shows in the last 90 days. The Platform does not block anyone automatically, but we may restrict an account that repeatedly abuses bookings.
- The Shop may cancel a confirmed booking only with a reason, which you receive in a message.

### 3.4 Inspection, quote and repair

- After the inspection, the Shop sends you the quote in the app, with each line and its price. You can **accept** it in full, **accept only some lines** or **refuse** it.
- The quote has a response deadline, shown on it. If you do not answer by then, the quote expires and the slot is released. The Shop may replace a sent quote with a new version or withdraw it; you always answer the latest version.
- When you accept (all or part), you accept the work and the price of those lines directly with the Shop. The lines and total of a sent quote can no longer be changed.
- If you refuse the quote, the Shop may charge the **inspection fee** shown on its page at the time of booking (it may also be 0 RON). You pay it directly to the Shop.
- When the job is finished, the Shop records the odometer reading, the work done and the cost. The car then shows as ready in the app.

### 3.5 Garage and alerts

Expiry dates for ITP, RCA and the vignette are optional. The alerts (30 days before, 7 days before and on the expiry date) are a help, not a guarantee: you remain responsible for your car’s documents.

### 3.6 Official history report

- For a car with jobs completed through Service-Hub you can buy an official PDF report, useful for example when selling the car. The price is shown before you pay (currently 29 RON, all taxes included).
- The report contains **only the jobs completed through Service-Hub**, not the car’s full history: the date, the shop and its city, the service and the accepted quote lines, the work, the odometer reading and the cost, plus the car details (make, model, year, license plate, VIN). It does not contain your name or phone. The data is what the Shops entered; we do not check or correct it. If the odometer readings do not increase from one job to the next, the report carries a note.
- You pay by card on Stripe’s secure page. The report is made as soon as the payment is confirmed and appears in Account → My reports, where you can download it free of charge as often as you like.
- **Right of withdrawal.** The report is digital content delivered immediately. Before you pay, we ask for your express consent for the report to be made immediately and your acknowledgment that you thereby lose the 14-day right of withdrawal (art. 16(m) of Government Emergency Ordinance 34/2014). If the report cannot be made, or is wrong because of the Platform, we refund you.
- Each report has a unique code. Anyone with the code can verify it at service-hub.ro/verifica; the page shows only the make, model and license plate, how many jobs the report has, the period and the report date, or that it was canceled. It does not show shops, amounts or anything about you. The report stays verifiable after you delete your account.
- A report is a snapshot as of the payment date. For later work, you get a new report. We may cancel a report obtained by fraud or made from wrong data; it can then no longer be downloaded, and the verification page shows “Canceled report.”
- The paid report is separate from the free export of your data (Account → My data), which always stays free and complete.

### 3.7 Reviews

- You can leave a review (1–5 stars and optional text) only for a completed job, once per booking, within 60 days of completion. Once sent, a review can no longer be changed.
- The review appears publicly on the Shop’s page with your first name and last initial (for example “Andrei M.”). The Shop may reply publicly.
- The review must describe your real experience. It may not contain insults, threats, other people’s personal data, advertising or false information.
- The Shop may report a review (fake, abusive, wrong shop, personal data). We check it within 5 working days; until then it stays visible. If it breaks the rules, we remove it from the list and from the score. The Shop cannot edit or delete a review.
- We do not pay or offer anything for reviews and do not order them in anyone’s interest. We check that each review comes from a customer with a completed job at that Shop.
