## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Service-Hub** is the controller of the account and Platform data.
- **The shop you book with** receives the data needed for the booking (section 4.1) and becomes, for that data, a separate controller: it uses it for the repair and for its own legal obligations (for example, its job records and the warranty).
- **Stripe**, which processes payments, is a separate controller for card data and for its own legal obligations (fraud prevention, anti-money laundering). Card data never reaches us.

## 2. What data we have

### 2.1 For every account

- your name, email address, phone, language (Romanian or English) and role (customer or shop);
- the account code (for example `C-00012`), when the account was created, when the email was confirmed and when you last used the app;
- the version of the Terms and of this policy you accepted, and when;
- your password, only in encrypted form (we cannot read it either);
- your choices in the app (for example, if you postponed the message about notifications).

### 2.2 If you are a Customer

- **Cars in your Garage:** make, model, year, license plate, VIN (if you enter it) and the expiry dates for ITP, RCA and the vignette (if you enter them).
- **Bookings:** the shop, service, day and time, your note, the status, plus a copy of your name, phone and car details at the time of booking.
- **Quotes and jobs:** the lines and prices, what you accepted or refused, the work done, the cost and the **odometer reading** recorded by the shop when finishing the job.
- Your **reviews** and **messages**; your **favorite** shops.
- **History reports** you bought: the code, the car, the jobs included, the price, the payment status and the PDF file.
- The number of **no-shows** in the last 90 days (calculated from bookings).

### 2.3 If you have a Shop account

- **Public profile:** the name, description, logo, workshop address and its map coordinates, phone numbers, website, Facebook page, year established, hours, closed days, booking rules, inspection fee and services offered.
- **Billing data:** legal name, tax ID, Trade Register number, registered office, VAT status, bank, IBAN, billing email and legal representative. Never public.
- **Subscription and payments:** status, start and end dates, price, number of paid colleagues, Stripe customer ID, amounts paid, payment dates and Stripe receipts.
- **Colleagues:** the email address you sent the invitation to, the colleague’s name and when they joined.
- **Phone confirmation:** the code sent by SMS is kept only in encrypted form and is valid for 10 minutes.
- **Preferences:** SMS on new request, daily summary.
- The shop’s bookings, quotes, jobs, reviews and messages, with the customer data from section 4.1.

### 2.4 Technical data

- **Push notifications** (only if you turn them on): your device’s technical address at the browser’s notification service, the encryption keys, the browser type and when the last notification was delivered.
- **Notification log:** which notification was sent, on which channel (push, email, SMS), when, and whether it arrived.
- **Error reports:** when something goes wrong, the app sends a technical report with the account’s internal ID, role, browser and system, the page (without the secret parts of the address) and the error. Emails, phone numbers and access codes in texts are removed before sending, and the IP address is not stored.
- **Server logs** of the hosting providers: IP address, time and page requested, kept by them for security.
- **Bot check** (Cloudflare Turnstile), at sign-up and sign-in: technical signals from the browser, to tell people from automated programs.

### 2.5 Location

- **Customers:** if you allow it in your browser, your phone tells the app your position so you can see nearby shops. **Your position is used only on your device**, to calculate distances. It is not sent to us and we do not keep it. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your browser settings.
- **Shops:** the workshop address is converted to coordinates through the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service (only the workshop address is sent). The “Use my current location” button saves the device’s position as the workshop location; like the address, it becomes public.

## 4. Who sees your data

### 4.1 Other users

- **The shop you book with** (the owner and colleagues) sees your name, phone, language and account code, the car in the booking (make, model, year, license plate, VIN), your note, your messages and your review, and, once the job is done, the odometer reading. If you have 3 or more no-shows in the last 90 days, it also sees that number.
- The shop **does not see** your email, your Garage, your ITP, RCA and vignette dates, or your bookings at other shops.
- When finishing a job, the shop receives the **last known odometer reading** for that license plate (the number only, from jobs done through the Platform at any shop), so that a lower reading is not entered by mistake or on purpose.
- **Reviews** are public to users of the Platform, with your first name and last initial (for example “Andrei M.”).
- **A history report code:** anyone who has it sees the car’s make, model and license plate, how many jobs the report has, the period and the report date.
- **A shop’s profile** is public to users of the Platform; its billing data is not.

### 4.2 The Service-Hub team

The Platform administrator sees the data needed for support, checking reported reviews, payments and stopping abuse. Every change made by the administrator is recorded in a log.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **While you have an account:** your account data, Garage, bookings, quotes, jobs, reviews and messages.
- **When you delete your account** (Account → My data → Delete account), deletion happens right away:
  - **Customer:** your account, Garage cars, favorites, notification devices and unsent notifications are deleted. Your name and phone are removed from bookings and conversations. What stays, without your name and phone: the jobs in the shops’ records (car, work, cost, odometer), your reviews (without a name) and the messages in the shops’ conversations. History reports you bought remain verifiable by code; they do not contain your name.
  - **Shop:** if the shop has no bookings and no payments, the account is deleted completely. If it has, the shop disappears from search, colleagues are removed, the owner’s name and phone are deleted and the account can no longer be used; billing data and payments are kept as long as accounting and tax law requires (currently up to 10 years), and the job history stays as a record.
- **Backups:** deleted data also disappears from backups within 30 days at most.
- **SMS confirmation codes:** valid for 10 minutes; kept only in encrypted form.
- **Technical data that prevents an action from being sent twice:** 7 days.
- **Error reports:** 90 days at most.
- **Server logs** of our providers: short periods, under their own rules.

## 6. How we keep data safe

- The database and files are in the European Union (Frankfurt). The connection to the app is always encrypted (HTTPS).
- Access rules are enforced in the database itself: a customer sees only their own data, a shop only its own shop’s data. Hiding a button is never the only protection.
- Passwords are kept only in encrypted form. Card details stay with Stripe.
- PDF reports are stored privately and can be downloaded only by their owner, after a check.
- Push notifications are encrypted all the way from us to your device.
- Error reports do not contain emails, phone numbers, access codes or IP addresses.
- If your data were exposed in a security incident, we would notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours, and you too if the risk to you is high.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **know** what data we hold about you and get a copy;
- **correct** wrong data (you change your name, phone and cars directly in the app);
- **erase** your data (in the app: Account → My data → Delete account), with the exceptions in section 5;
- ask us to **restrict** the use of your data;
- **object** to the use of your data based on legitimate interest;
- **data portability**: the file from Account → My data → Download my data contains, in a structured format (JSON), your account, cars, bookings, quotes, reviews and messages; it is free;
- **withdraw your consent** for push notifications (in Account or in your browser settings) and for location (in your browser settings), without affecting what was done before;
- **complain** to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru nr. 28-30, sector 1, Bucharest, [dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro).

For any request, write to us at {{email}} from your account’s email address. We answer within one month (in complex cases we may extend this by two more months, with an explanation). For data a shop keeps in its own records, you can also contact the shop; we will help you get in touch.
